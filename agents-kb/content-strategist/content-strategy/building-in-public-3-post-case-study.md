# Building in Public - 3-Post Case Study Template

**Source**: https://www.tiktok.com/@personalbrandlaunch0/video/7662094174128262431 (also: https://www.instagram.com/reel/Der-4R4B4jh/ - same video, frames add the on-screen templates and series examples)
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-07-17

## Core Insight
A viral "building a business from scratch" account followed a repeatable 3-post template that turns viewers into invested followers who then become buyers, because they helped build the product and trust the founder before the launch.

## How It Works
- **Post 1, Personal intro video**: introduce who you are, what you've done, where you're at, and where you're going; state your dream/mission.
- **Post 2, Business intro video**: use the underdog approach; name the small-fish-in-a-big-industry angle, the current problems in the industry, and the downside of being new; reintroduce your mission/goal and break down the business in more depth.
- **Post 3+, Milestones/updates series**: each video is a new step of building the business (logos, designs, first samples, etc.); works either as a daily series or a weekly milestone/update cadence, both proven to work.
- **Templates on screen (frames of the IG version)**: Post 1 is scripted with one of three story templates, **Hero's Journey**, **Man in a Hole** or **About Me**; Post 2 with **The Big Goal / Dream** or **Challenge to Victory**. **Challenge to Victory is not in that file** (its "The Challenge" is the "Is it possible to X in Y time?" experiment, a different arc). Slots as read off the frame: X days/months/years ago someone told me I'd never get (dream result, 3-5 words) / I was doing (actions, 5-10 words) / and still getting (bad result, 3-5 words) / until I realized (realization, 5-10 words) / I followed this 3-step framework: Step #1, #2, #3 (step name in 1-3 words + a 10-15 word actionable, measurable breakdown each) / I went from (before state) to (after state) / and ended up (positive side effect of the dream result, 5-10 words) / (dream result) is not hard, you are probably just doing the wrong things to get there.
- **Series cadence examples shown**: daily series, @chida.rajan "Building Luxury Cabin Journey" (24 episodes shown, views from about 59K to 1.6M); weekly milestone/update series, @rileyrehl "Becoming Series" (EP1 Blood Test 151K ... EP7 Building Healthy Habits 7.3M). Each episode is one concrete step, titled as a numbered episode.
- This series builds toward a **future launch post**: by then the audience already voted on decisions, watched the journey and behind-the-scenes, and built a personal connection, so they trust the founder and want to buy.

## Example
A new account documented starting a business from zero: personal intro → business intro (underdog framing) → ongoing build-in-public update series (logos, designs, samples) → launch post to an audience that helped shape the product.

## When to Use
Starting a personal brand or product business from scratch on Instagram/TikTok and want to build a warm, trusting audience before you have anything to sell.
