# My Video vs. My Script Format

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYhP26ewTAQ/?igsh=MTh4cGpyMHpjNzhmdw== (also: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdpCJjOxJfa/)
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-05-24

## Core Insight
Showing "my video vs. my script" side-by-side proves that viral content is planned, not spontaneous, and turns the process itself into a CTA magnet (comment for the script).

## How It Works
- Film the final polished video
- Show the raw script alongside it in split-screen or cut format
- The contrast between messy script and polished output creates curiosity
- CTA embedded: "Comment 'SCRIPT' and I'll send it over", turns viewers into leads
- The script document itself follows a fixed breakdown table: Visual Hook, Verbal Hook, Rewatch Trigger, then positive/emotional/motivating beats and a personal/relatable moment, each row phrased as a question with linked example answers, ending in a CTA row

## Example
@personalbrandlaunch shows their scripted videos next to the final output, then offers to DM the scripts to anyone who comments, growing both engagement and a DM list in one reel. A later version of the same format paired 4 video types with their scripts in one reel: a "Shock" video (2.5M views), a "Rating/Hashtags" video (1.6M), a trend-jacking video (1.5M), and a "Followers" growth video (1.4M), each screenshotted next to its script breakdown table.

## When to Use
When you want to demystify your content process AND generate DM conversations at the same time. Works best when your final video looks much more polished than the raw script would suggest.
