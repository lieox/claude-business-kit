# Social Media Strategy Deck: The Full Structure, Page by Page

**Source**: https://vt.tiktok.com/ZS9v74CBQ/ (Sara, @volumeonestudios, TikTok, 28.04.2026, 2:32, ~144K views). Read from the transcript AND from the deck pages visible in the frames. Her full 52-page template is comment-gated ("STRATEGY") and was not obtained.
**Category**: content-strategy
**Added**: 2026-10-31

## Core Insight
A strategy deck should never be built from scratch twice. One fixed structure, filled per brand, so the time goes into the strategy itself and not into the slides. Her rule at the end: don't spend hours designing it.

## The Pages, In Order

1. **Brand overview.** The most important thing on the page is the **USP**. It should already be answered in the new-client questionnaire.
2. **Tone of voice, on 8 sliders** (not "casual or formal", because no brand is 100% one side). The pairs, exactly as on her slide:
   - Casual ↔ Formal
   - Friendly ↔ Professional
   - High-energy ↔ Reserved
   - Fun ↔ Serious
   - Accessible ↔ Exclusive
   - Relational ↔ Transactional
   - Traditional ↔ Nontraditional
   - Community ↔ Individualistic
   Plus a short written description under the scale.
3. **Social media goals,** with the **KPIs matched to each goal** so progress is tracked against what the client actually asked for.
4. **Target audience, the page she spends the most time on:**
   - **Demographics.**
   - **Psychographics:** what leads them to a buying decision.
   - **A named primary persona card** (photo, name, age, gender, location, occupation, marital status) with these prompts: **Interests** (list as many as you can, it shapes the content) · **Challenges** (the biggest thing they struggle with, the problem they want solved) · **Behaviors** (most days, free time, days off) · **Motivation** (goals as they relate to the challenges) · **Influences** (who influences them online, friends, community, family). More personas case by case, because "when you speak to everyone you speak to no one".
5. **Competitor overview,** kept short ("clients are a category of one"). One page per competitor: followers per platform, **Who they are** (background), **How they compete** (what they sell that directly competes, is it better), **Unique selling point,** and what the client admires about them but wants to do better.
6. **Audit, one per platform in the package.** A checklist beside a screenshot of the profile: **username, profile category, profile picture, bio,** and everything you would change while working together so the profile is optimized.
7. **Strategy, one per platform** (her template: Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest), because every platform is different.
8. **Content pillars:** the topic buckets the content rotates through. Skip the page if pillars are not used.
9. **Example posting schedule:** Monday to Friday, one line per platform per day ("Instagram: content piece · TikTok: content piece").

## When to Use
Building or refreshing the business's social media strategy, page by page.
