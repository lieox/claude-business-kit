## Core Insight
Knowing what "okay," "good," and "outlier" look like for each metric lets you identify which videos deserve to be doubled down on, and which to stop repeating.

## How It Works

**Views-to-follower-count ratio:**
- 10% = okay
- 25%+ = good
- 5× followers = outlier → double down immediately

**View-to-follow ratio (optimizing for followers):**
- 0.1% = okay
- 0.5-1% = good
- 2%+ = outlier → use this video topic/structure often

**View-to-ManyChat-comment ratio (optimizing for leads):**
- 0.3% = okay
- 0.5-1% = good
- 2%+ = outlier → use this CTA often

**Watch time (optimizing for views/retention):**
- 30% or less = okay
- 40-50% = good
- 70%+ = outlier → repeat the topic or structure

**Likes-to-views ratio:**
- 3-4% = okay
- 4-7% = good
- 7-12% = outlier → people clearly love this topic

## Bad / Good / Great: Instagram Insights Per-View Rates (@personalbrandlaunch, 2026-10-19)
Each tier is a real Instagram Insights card shown on screen ("this is bad / good / great X"), so treat the numbers as example reference points, not official thresholds. All rates are per view.

| Metric | Bad | Good | Great |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who viewed: followers / non-followers | 79.5% / 20.5% | 58.5% / 41.5% | 25.9% / 74.1% |
| Skip rate (lower is better) | 68.9% | 36.0% | 21.4% |
| Follows per view | 0.1% | 0.5% | 1% |
| Repost rate | 0.05% | 0.8% | 2.0% |
| Save rate | 0.2% | 2.1% | 4.0% |
| Share rate | 0.1% | 1.5% | 4.3% |
| Like rate | 0.8% | 3.2% | 7.4% |
| Comment rate | 0.1% | 0.4% | 3.0% |

- **Watch time / retention graph** (no numbers on screen): bad = the curve drops steeply in the first seconds and stays low; good = gradual decline; great = a nearly flat curve that holds most viewers to the end.
- **Non-follower share is the reach signal:** a "great" reel is seen ~3:1 by non-followers. A reel seen mostly by followers (~80%) is not being pushed beyond the existing audience.
- **Agreement with the tiers above:** follows per view matches (0.1% okay, 0.5-1% good); her "great" 1% sits at the top of "good" here, below the 2%+ "outlier" bar. Like rate also lines up (7.4% "great" falls in the 7-12% outlier band).
- Caveat: early-drop rates such as skip rate and p25 are not comparable across reels of very different length, because a short reel reaches each checkpoint faster. Compare reels of similar length.

## 4-Step Action Framework
1. Choose your goal: followers, leads, or views
2. Find your posts that hit the "outlier" benchmark for that goal
3. Double down on those videos (topic, structure, CTA)
4. Repeat

## When to Use
Monthly content audit. Pull your last 30 videos, score each against these benchmarks, identify outliers, plan your next month's doubles.
