# Viral Storytelling Videos Run on Templatized Structures

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZ5mFb9slKT/ (also: https://www.instagram.com/p/De2lNC8DueQ/ - same breakdown as a 10-slide carousel, with every template written out in full)
**Category**: copywriting
**Added**: 2026-06-23 (updated 2026-10-25)

## Core Insight
Every viral storytelling video looks spontaneous but is built on a reusable, templatized story structure. Pull the structure out, fill it with your own story, and you can replicate the result.

## How It Works
- For each viral story video she shows the underlying skeleton: an opening line that hooks, then the same fill-in-the-blank template arc behind it.
- Named structures shown: Hero's Journey (Intro, Inflection Point, Rising Action, Climax, Falling Action, Resolution and CTA), Man in a Hole (Comfort Zone, Trigger, Crisis, Recovery, Better Place), a Problem/Breakthrough/Fix arc, and a Doubt/Initial Struggle/Turning Point/Solution/Transformation/Myth Bust arc.
- Each template has slots like "X days/years ago I was a normal [role]" and "until I realized [realization]," ending in a resolution plus a call to action to your business or service.
- The takeaway: the opening line is just a hook, the views come from following a proven arc you keep reusing.

## Example
She pairs strong opening lines with their structure: "You want to know why we started our business? Spite." / "Yesterday I was at the park when I noticed something weird" (22.7M, Man in a Hole) / "A year ago I was reading my Bible front to back and praying every night" (11.2M, Problem-Breakthrough-Fix) / "Is it possible to write a successful college essay for Harvard in just one hour?" (1.5M, Doubt-to-Transformation). Each plays over the matching template card.

### The Breakthrough (Problem → Breakthrough → Fix)
1. **PROBLEM** (state the problem): "I was doing (solution/action) and still getting (bad result). But even after trying (action/actions) I kept (bad result)."
2. **BREAKTHROUGH** (a mind-blowing realization or result), pick one:
   - Option 1: "Until I realized (realization)"
   - Option 2: "Until I did this one thing that got me (result)"
3. **FIX (TAKEAWAY)** (break down what got you from problem to breakthrough), pick one:
   - Option 1, found-resource story: "One day I was doing (action) when I came across (resource) that explained (solution/realization). Since I had nothing to lose, I tried (solution implemented) and X days/months/years later (dream result/social proof/positive outcomes)."
   - Option 2, steps breakdown: "Before I struggled (getting dream result) because of (problem #1), (problem #2), and (problem #3) and I had no idea how to do (action). But in order to achieve (dream result) you actually want to (solution), so here are the steps we followed. First (step #1), then (step #2), and lastly (step #3)."

### Challenge to Victory (Doubt → Initial Struggle → Turning Point → Solution → Transformation → Myth Bust)
1. **DOUBT** (limiting belief or challenge): "X days/months/years ago someone/society told me I'd never get (dream result)"
2. **INITIAL STRUGGLE** (problem + effort put in): "I was doing (action/actions) and still getting (bad result)."
3. **THE TURNING POINT** (the one thing that changed everything): "Until I realized (realization, e.g. how the College Board actually designs their questions)"
4. **THE SOLUTION** (method, framework, approach), pick one:
   - Option 1, acronym: "I followed the (acronym you created) method. (X letter) stands for (step) (breakdown step)." Repeat for each letter.
   - Option 2, numbered: "I followed this (#) step framework. Step #1. (step) (breakdown step)." Repeat for each step.
5. **THE TRANSFORMATION** (before and after): "I went from (before) to (after) and ended up (positive side effect/effects from transformation)."
6. **MYTH BUST** (challenge a belief the audience holds): "(dream result) is not hard, you are probably just doing the wrong things to get there."

Word-count guidance per slot for this structure (3-5 words for results, 10-15 for each step breakdown) is in `content-strategy/building-in-public-3-post-case-study.md`.

Then per structure, a pair: a looping clip slide (structure name, example, stage chips) followed by a full template slide. Last slide: "Want it done for you?" with 3 client before/after follower counts (8,741 → 44.5K, 3,071 → 85.1K, 1,460 → 45K) and a "comment SMM" keyword CTA.

## When to Use
When scripting a story-based reel. Pick a proven structure (Hero's Journey, Man in a Hole, Doubt-to-Transformation, etc.), drop your own story into the slots, and open with a strong hook line so the arc carries the rest.
