# 100 vs. 100K vs. 1M View Hook Rewrites

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DVwDhiAjtPZ/ (also: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdR1nxYoI7R/ - hook construction mechanics by @personalbrandlaunch)
**Category**: hooks-and-openers
**Added**: 2026-04-07
**Updated**: 2026-09-15 - added hook construction mechanics (sourcing, count, delivery, audience breadth, written style)

## Core Insight
The same topic can generate 100 views or 1M views based purely on how the hook is written. Generic/informational hooks get 100 views; emotional stakes, contrarian framing, or personal narrative get 1M.

## How It Works
The progression across all niches follows the same pattern:
- **100 views**, generic, informational, no tension ("Here are the top X funds everyone should know")
- **100K views**, specific + audience identity ("To every girl who ever thought 'I don't know how to invest'...")
- **1M views**, emotional stakes, contrarian framing, or personal narrative ("If I were in my 20s again, I'd do this every paycheck so I don't end up broke")

## Example

**Marriage niche:**
- 100 views: "Can we talk about one of the hardest things in marriage?"
- 100K views: "Welcome to How to Treat Your Pregnant Wife or Girlfriend, Volume 1"
- 1M views: "What do you do if you don't like your wife?"

**Food/health niche:**
- 100 views: "Hey, I want to cook an easy cheat meal with me"
- 100K views: "Eating healthy is expensive. Here's how to meal prep lunches for the week with just $20"
- 1M views: "People think building muscle is expensive, but I'll show you how to do it for $8 a day"

**Finance niche:**
- 100 views: "Here are the top Vanguard index funds that everyone should know about"
- 100K views: "To every girl who ever thought 'I don't know how to invest in a mutual fund,' this video is for you"
- 1M views: "So if I were in my 20s or early 30s again, I would do this every single paycheck so I don't end up broke"

## Hook Construction Mechanics (companion breakdown)
A different creator applies the same 100 / 100K / 1M tier system, but to HOW the hook gets built rather than what it says:
- **Sourcing**, 100-view: freestyled, no research; 100K/1M: researched outlier hooks in-niche that follow the 5x rule (a hook that got 5x the account's normal views)
- **Hook count**, 100-view: one hook; 100K: two hooks; 1M: three hooks stacked together
- **Verbal delivery**, 100-view: sitting and talking to camera; 1M: performing a visual hook (acting it out, not just narrating)
- **Visual hook type**, 1M: built from a talking-form base plus a clone, movement, or prop element layered on top
- **Audience breadth**, 100-view: hook is niche, speaks only to the target audience; 1M: hook is super broad, appeals to a ton of people so they don't scroll past
- **Written hook**, 100-view: just the caption text; 1M: big bold all-caps text overlay above the subject's head

## When to Use
When writing or reviewing any hook, run it through the 3-tier test. If it sounds like the 100-view version, rewrite it with emotional stakes, a contrarian premise, or a specific personal narrative before posting. Then check the construction mechanics too: source outlier hooks (5x rule), stack 2-3 hooks, perform rather than narrate, broaden the audience appeal, and go bold with the written overlay.
