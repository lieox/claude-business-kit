# The Outcome + 3 Pain Points + Intro to Solution Hook

**Source**: https://www.tiktok.com/@personalbrandlaunch0/video/7686106611609308429 (@personalbrandlaunch0)
**Category**: hooks-and-openers
**Added**: 2026-10-27

## Core Insight
A 3-part problem-solution hook that keeps going viral across unrelated niches: name the outcome the viewer wants, fire three pain points back to back, then hand off to the solution. Three videos from hair, dog training and baking use completely different words but the same skeleton, and all of them performed well. The structure carries the hook, not the wording.

## How It Works
- **Outcome**: open on the result or situation the viewer wants ("If giving yourself a blowout...", "If you want a dog who will...", "Did you know you can start your own bakery from home...")
- **3 pain points, back to back**: three short clauses with no connective tissue, each one a frustration or obstacle ("pulls out too much hair, takes too much time, and ultimately just pisses you off"). Phrased either as what goes wrong now, or as "without [obstacle], [obstacle], or [obstacle]"
- **Intro to solution**: one line that hands off to the body of the video ("here's how I cheated", "you are going to love this behavior", "Hi, I'm Jess and I'm in the middle of...")
- The whole hook is one sentence or two. The pain points are what make the viewer recognize themselves before the value starts.

## Example
- **@rachel.ocool** (1.6M views, hair): "If giving yourself a blowout [outcome] pulls out too much hair, takes too much time, and ultimately just pisses you off [3 pain points], here's how I cheated [intro to solution]."
- **@thepainfreepettrainer** (2.3M views, dog training): "If you want a dog who will quietly sit and wait like this [outcome], instead of jumping up, getting distracted or demand-barking [3 pain points], you are going to love this behavior [intro to solution]."
- **@microbakerygirl** (416K views, baking): "Did you know that you can start your very own bakery from home [outcome] without a storefront, high startup costs, or years of experience? [3 pain points] Hi, I'm Jess and I'm in the middle of moving my micro bakery... [intro to solution]"

## When to Use
Tutorials, how-tos and "here's the method" videos where the audience already knows the problem (awareness level 3: problem-aware). Swap in the niche's three most common complaints.
