# Written Hooks 101 - Six Formatting Rules

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZkMXSbh-WH/ (also: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdL77HFoHeF/)
**Category**: hooks-and-openers
**Added**: 2026-06-16 (updated 2026-09-14)

## Core Insight
The on-screen text hook can make or break a regular video, and small formatting choices change readability and stopping power. Be extremely intentional about how the written hook looks, not just what it says, both its text formatting (case, line breaks, wording) and its visual production (fonts, color, motion, sound).

## How It Works
Text formatting:
- **Capitalization**: Use Title Case (capital letter at the start of each word) rather than ALL CAPS, easier to read and looks more intentional.
- **Two-line split**: Divide the hook into two lines instead of one long line, much easier to scan in the first second.
- **Delete filler words**: Cut every word that isn't essential; keep only the words that need to be there so the hook reads instantly.

Visual production (don't leave it plain):
- **Layer fonts**: don't use one boring font, combine a big bold or cursive font with a normal/simple font for the rest of the text.
- **Brand it visually**: don't keep it plain white text, use your brand colors plus PNGs that represent what the video is actually about.
- **Add motion and sound**: don't leave the hook static and silent, add a sound effect and text animations so it moves and lands with the audio.

## Example
Eva runs three A/B comparisons on the text side: Title Case vs ALL CAPS (Title Case wins), one line vs two lines (two lines wins because it's easier to read), and full sentence vs filler-words-removed (the trimmed version wins). She credits the creator whose video reminded her of these points. A separate reel stacks the three production rules as one closing formula: "add them all together and you got the perfect hook."

## When to Use
Whenever you write AND design the on-screen text hook for a Reel/TikTok: apply Title Case, a two-line split, and ruthless filler-word deletion to the wording, then layer fonts, brand colors/PNGs, and a sound effect + animation to the visual production before posting.
