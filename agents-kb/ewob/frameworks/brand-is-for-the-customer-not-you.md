# Brand Is for the Customer, Not for You

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbrBP2jyLeJ/
**Category**: frameworks
**Added**: 2026-08-08

## Core Insight
A brand/logo isn't meant to feel like the founder - it's meant to feel like the "entity" or "character" the target customer needs it to feel like. Personal taste is the wrong filter for brand and design decisions.

## How It Works
- The instinct "I want my brand to feel like me" is the wrong starting question.
- The right question: who does the customer need this brand to *be* for them (what character/entity)?
- Design decisions (visual identity, tone, layout) should be reverse-engineered from the target customer's expectations, not the owner's/designer's personal preference.

## Example
Christina Hall (@superlunardesign) delivers it as a blunt correction: "Your brand, your logo... not about you. Oh, but I want my brand to feel like me. No, you don't... You want your brand to feel like the person, the entity, the character that your customers need it to feel like."

## When to Use
When scoping a client site/landing page and the client is tempted to make a design call based on personal taste - pull the decision back to "what does the target customer need this to feel like" instead. Useful as a gut-check during the Visual Identity Gate / DESIGN.md selection step.
