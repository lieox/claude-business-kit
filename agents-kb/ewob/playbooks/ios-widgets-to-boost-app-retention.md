# iOS Widgets to Boost App Opens and Retention

**Source**: https://vt.tiktok.com/ZSCJGQ2jy/
**Category**: playbooks
**Added**: 2026-06-23

## Core Insight
Users want to use your app but keep getting distracted by a hundred push notifications a day. Shipping an iOS home-screen widget makes your app stand out and drives a significant increase in app opens, because the widget occupies prime real estate and builds a glance-and-open habit.

## How It Works
- A widget takes up at minimum four app slots on the home screen, so it is visually dominant and hard to ignore.
- The mechanism is habit, not interruption: if users build the habit of unlocking their phone and glancing at your widget for information, that habit directly improves retention.
- Standing out passively on the home screen beats competing in the notification stream.
- Bonus lever: also ship a lock-screen widget. Only up to four apps can live on the lock screen, so being one of them gives a significantly higher chance users remember to open your app.

## Example
The creator says he has used the iOS-widget tactic across all of his apps to stand out against the flood of daily push notifications, and points to widgets driving more app opens because of how prominent they are on the home screen.

## When to Use
When building or marketing a mobile app (especially iOS / Swift) and you need to improve retention and daily app opens. Add a home-screen widget that surfaces useful at-a-glance information, and add a lock-screen widget to claim one of the four scarce lock-screen slots.
