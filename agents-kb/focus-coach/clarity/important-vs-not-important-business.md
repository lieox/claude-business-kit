# Important vs Not Important When Growing a Business

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DX9MjZPhAKS/
**Category**: clarity
**Added**: 2026-05-17

## Core Insight
Most founders waste time on what looks like business but isn't business. Clarity comes from sorting every activity into "important" or "not important" by whether it directly drives sales, and ruthlessly cutting the rest.

## How It Works
- **Not important**: logo, brand colors, fancy website, business cards
- **Important**: clear offer (a bad offer won't sell no matter what), landing page with offers + social proof + CTA (the actual sales surface, not a website), inbound + outbound marketing (awareness for the offer), sales (no sales = no money)
- **Very important** for scale: SOPs / systems / processes (can't scale a one-person op well without them), hiring and delegating (eventually required to grow past yourself)
- The rule: if it doesn't lead to a sale or let a sale repeat at scale, it's optional decoration

## Example
She built her business for over 18 months without a logo. Doesn't have business cards. Doesn't really use her website, only her landing page sells.

## When to Use
When a founder is stuck in branding/website-perfecting paralysis instead of building offer + landing page + marketing + sales. Use as a quick triage filter on any "should I do X?" question.
