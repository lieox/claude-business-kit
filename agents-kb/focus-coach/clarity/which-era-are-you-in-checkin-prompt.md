## Core Insight
A simple two-part self-reflection question used as an engagement/check-in post: "Which era are you in, and what's your next move?", prompts the audience to name their current life/business chapter before committing to the next step.

## How It Works
- Ask the audience to first name where they currently are (their "era").
- Then ask what their next move is, tying identity/stage awareness directly to forward action.
