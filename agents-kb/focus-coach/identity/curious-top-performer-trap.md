# The Curious Top-Performer Trap (Generalist Edition)

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DQFRzN2jyhV/
**Category**: identity
**Added**: 2026-05-17

## Core Insight
Curiosity is widely called the biggest green flag of a top performer, but for the multi-passionate generalist, being a curious top performer is also a fast track to burnout and resentment. You become the Swiss Army knife everyone taps in, which feels validating but quietly hands over control of your career to other people.

## How It Works
- Insatiably curious people are almost never specialists, they're generalists, chameleons who can plug in anywhere
- You elevate every room because you ask questions and challenge thinking → people always want you in the room
- That makes you the "person everyone wants" → you say yes → you let other people decide how you spend your time and energy
- The cost: you never figure out where you actually shine, so you can't self-advocate. Other people make career decisions *for* you based on what they think you're capable of
- Result: underpaid even in a high-paying job, overworked because you solve everyone's problem, career happens *to* you
- The fix is 4 parts: (1) own your skill set, exactly how it fits and how to leverage it (2) know your brand, uniquely differentiated positioning (3) market yourself, excellence isn't enough, self-advocate for what you want and deserve (4) act in your own best interest, top performers act without permission and take risks

## When to Use
For any high-performing generalist quietly burning out, especially the one whose calendar is full of other people's priorities. As a frame to legitimize "stop saying yes and start positioning" before any career or business design work.
