# The "I Don't Want a Job, I Want..." Manifesto

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/p/DdUH-5FFP2_/ (also: https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dery2sYtw-i/)
**Updated**: 2026-10-19 (spoken reel version + the 3-step "how" from its caption)
**Category**: identity
**Added**: 2026-09-16

## Core Insight
A six-beat "I don't want X, I want Y" contrast carousel that rejects the default career script (a job, the status quo, one lane, the American Dream, a boss, a cubicle) point-by-point in favor of a self-defined, multifaceted, ownership-driven life; each rejection is paired with a specific, named alternative rather than a vague "more freedom."

## How It Works
Six parallel contrast statements, each following "I don't want X. I want Y.":
- Job → a multifaceted web of opportunities and creative side quests that light your soul on fire and make you rich
- The status quo → a life so juicy and uniquely yours that you're awestruck by your own existence
- Doing ONE thing → learning forever, chasing curiosities, reinventing yourself again and again
- The "American Dream" → actual freedom, where *you* decide what success and a rich life mean
- A boss dictating your worth → calling all the shots, total ownership over your brilliance and how big you go with it
- Being hidden away in a cubicle → being SEEN, making a positive impact, building community, shaping the future
Closes on a single rallying line: "It's time to break the d*mn rules and build the rich AF, creative life you truly want. Let's f**king go."

## The "How": 3 Steps to Design a Rich AF, Creative Life (reel caption, 2026-10-19)
The reel pairs the manifesto ("what I want") with a three-step "how":
1. **Dare to dream big.** Hold the big vision without tempering it or adding a "but" to make it more realistic. Realism belongs to the execution phase, not the dreaming phase.
2. **Believe it before you see it.** A big life means the path less traveled, so you have to fiercely believe it is possible and that you are the person who can build it. "Unshakable belief breeds motivation and resilience to keep going."
3. **Know your unique genius.** Your "magic sauce": the combination of inherent skills, strengths and aura where you naturally excel, and the reason people are drawn to you. Operating there = peak performance, fulfillment, magnetism, best ideas, "and THAT is when the abundance flows."
Payoff: total confidence + self-trust + dialed into your unique genius = "unstoppable"; you own your time, your creativity and your earning power, and you get to have fun building it.

## Spoken Version (reel, 2026-10-19)
Delivered to camera from the car as a first-person turning-point declaration: "My whole life changed when I definitively owned that I don't want a f***ing job. I don't want a boss. I don't want anyone telling me no." Then the "I want" side: build things that didn't exist before, honor being multi-passionate, exist in "my creative brilliance, my unique genius, my magic sauce," attract cool people, chase curiosity, "create a whole bunch of side quests and get rich while I do it all." Closes: "I'm gonna go big with it because I am a larger than life person and I cannot be contained. Go big or go freaking home." New detail vs the carousel: the change is framed as a single moment of *definitively owning* the decision, not a gradual drift.

## Example
"I don't want the 'American Dream.' I want actual freedom. Where *I* decide what success means and what a rich life looks like FOR me." / "I don't want a boss dictating my worth. I want to call ALL the shots. I want total ownership over my brilliance and how BIG I go with it."

## When to Use
As a shareable identity-declaration/manifesto piece for a multi-passionate audience who already senses the default script doesn't fit; works as a standalone values-clarifier before any business-design work (pairs with [[golden-handcuffs-vs-big-life-thats-yours]] and [[what-if-you-went-bigger]], same rejection-of-the-impressive-but-trapped-life theme, here compressed into six punchy contrast pairs instead of narrative form).
