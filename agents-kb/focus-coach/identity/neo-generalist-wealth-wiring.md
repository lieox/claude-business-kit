# Neo-Generalist Wealth Wiring

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DW4yUoujRqz/?igsh=MXZqOTRrZWx3N2V6ag== (also: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdCGUbatOq8/, https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdW44YNNXFt/)
**Category**: identity

## Core Insight
Multi-passionate people are neo-generalists, not just jacks of all trades, but people with range AND depth AND mastery in a few high-value areas. These traits (non-conformist, big thinker, creative problem-solver) are the exact traits of the most successful people in history. The barrier isn't ability, it's environment and intentionality.

## How It Works
- **Reframe the identity**: You're not "too much" or "unfocused", you're a neo-generalist with range and depth
- **Name the traits**: non-conformist, challenges status quo, sees possibility others don't, realistic optimist, creative problem-solver, these are assets, not liabilities
- **Identify the block**: Playing in sandboxes that require you to be a "well-mannered neurotypical specialist" kills your earning potential
- **The prescription**: Choose environments and people who celebrate your creativity and want in on your perspective
- **Design intentionally**: What you do, how you do it, who you do it with, all three must align

## Example
Annie references Steve Jobs and Beyoncé as examples of people who possessed these exact neo-generalist traits. The framing: they didn't succeed despite their non-conformism, they succeeded because of it.

## When to Use
When a client questions whether their range of interests is a weakness or feels stuck in a career that pays well but doesn't fit. This is the reframe: your unique genius = the skills where you deliver the most value with what feels like the least effort, and that's what commands premium pay.
