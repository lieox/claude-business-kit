## Core Insight
The multi-passionate, multi-faceted creative has spent years being told they're "too much," so they played by the rules and stuffed themselves into boxes that were always too small. The reframe: your "too much" is the exact right amount for the people who want to be led by you. This is a permission slip to stop shrinking and expand into your bigness.

## How It Works
- **Name the pattern:** you're larger than life, bursting with ideas, and have been accused of being "too much," so you shrank to fit expectations and became "the disruptor within the box."
- **Validate the cost:** your confidence has taken hits in a world that demands you be someone you're not and wants you to shrink.
- **Grant permission:** consciously give yourself permission to expand into your bigness instead of waiting for the world to allow it.
- **Reframe "too much":** your bigness is exactly right for the people who want to be in your community, to be led, influenced, and impacted by you.
- **Act on it:** be multi-faceted, vibrant, and totally untamed. You're a change-maker here to set the tone for what's possible.

## Example
Annie speaks directly to the viewer: "Your 'too much' is the exact right amount for the people who want to be you... who want to be led, influenced, and impacted by you." She closes with permission and a rallying push: "So go on. Be big... Because you are a change maker." (Caption energy: "You ARE big, SO BE BIG. Nothing remarkable ever came from being normal.")

## When to Use
When you (or a multi-passionate client) feel pressure to shrink, tone yourself down, or pick one lane to be more "acceptable." Use it as an identity reset: the trait you were shamed for is the magnet for your right audience.
