# What If You Went Bigger? - The Multi-Passionate Paradox

**Source**: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYUjtlxOqFS/ (also: https://www.instagram.com/p/Da5hM84DvDD/)
**Category**: identity
**Added**: 2026-05-17

## Core Insight
The high-achieving multi-passionate gets stuck in a paradox: outwardly successful (impressive titles, ladder climbed), inwardly unfulfilled, fragmented, and quietly resentful, because all that striving went into other people's goals while saying "yes" to everything out of desperation for meaning.

## How It Works
The trap progression:
- Never fit a linear career path → questioned yourself → set out to "do everything right"
- Became the striver → climbed the ladder → earned the titles → became a people-pleaser spread too thin
- Insatiable need to learn and create → say yes to every opportunity → energy fragmented in many directions, significant progress in none
- The paradox: recognize you haven't scratched the surface of what's possible, while urging yourself to stay in the box and "just be grateful"
The unlock, five "what if" reframes:
- What if you finally admitted you want more?
- What if you owned that you want to design your career *for you* instead of letting it happen *to you*?
- What if you stopped handing your energy out to everyone around you?
- What if you funneled all of it toward your own goal?
- What if 100% of your time was spent on what you actually want?

## When to Use
For a multi-passionate client who is "doing fine on paper" but quietly drained. As the awakening moment before any positioning / business-design work, the goals can't be set until the paradox is named and the box is rejected.
