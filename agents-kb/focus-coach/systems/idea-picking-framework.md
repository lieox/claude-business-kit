# The Idea-Picking Framework: 3 Checklists Before You Commit

**Source**: https://www.tiktok.com/@multipassionatemadison/video/7664653764330081567
**Category**: systems
**Added**: 2026-07-24

## Core Insight
Multi-passionate people fail by picking ideas too soon, letting excitement (not logic) fuel the decision, which backfires into things that aren't sustainable or right for the season. The fix is a repeatable 3-question checklist you run every idea through, plus a deliberate marinate period before you act.

## How It Works
Let each idea "marinate" first (a week or two), then run it through three checklists in order:
- **1. Is it sustainable?** (most important) - Does this fit everything already going on in your life right now (travel phase, existing projects)? Will it support them or steal from them? Will it stress you out? Can you sustain it for 6-12 months, or 2+ years, depending on the idea's scope?
- **2. Is it still a passion after it marinates?** - Excitement fuels decisions over logic, so wait a week or two and check if you still care. Often what survived the wait was quietly a good idea all along.
- **3. Is it profitable?** - Not every idea has to make money (some are just for fun), but for the ones that should, study the industry and confirm there's a real path to profit before committing.

## Example
The creator keeps a running list of ideas in her Notes app. Her recurring failure was "picking and executing too soon" on excitement alone, ending up in projects she wasn't excited about or that weren't sustainable. Running each idea through the three checklists (with a marinate delay) is how she now decides what to actually commit to.

## When to Use
When you have more ideas than time (the classic multi-passionate overwhelm) and need a logical filter to decide which few to actually pursue, instead of chasing whatever feels most exciting in the moment.
